Apple Health Sync (iPhone Shortcuts)

Automatically sync your morning scale weight (kg), body fat %, BMI, lean mass, and sleep from your iPhone to TrackIt.

How to automate with iOS Shortcuts:
  1. Open the built-in Shortcuts app on your iPhone.
  2. Tap Automation → New Automation → When Wake-up Alarm is Stopped.
  3. Action 1: Find Health Samples (Type: Weight in kg, Sort: Most Recent, Limit: 1).
  4. Action 2: Find Health Samples (Type: Body Fat %, Limit: 1).
  5. Action 3: Find Health Samples (Type: Sleep Analysis, Most Recent 1 night).
  6. Action 4: Get Contents of URL → Method: POST to your Webhook URL with JSON payload: {"token": "YOUR_TOKEN", "weight_kg": [Weight], "body_fat_pct": [Body Fat], "sleep_hours": [Sleep]}
